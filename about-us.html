About Millbrook Manor Nursing Home

A welcoming residential care environment where dignity, respect, comfort and individual well-being remain at the heart of everything we do.

Welcome to Millbrook Manor

Welcome to Millbrook Manor Nursing Home, a private residential care facility located in the peaceful surroundings of Saggart, County Dublin.

We are committed to providing person-centred care in a safe, warm and supportive environment. Our approach recognises that every resident is an individual, with their own life story, preferences, routines, relationships and aspirations.

Our aim is to support residents to live meaningful and fulfilling lives while receiving the level of care and assistance they require.

Our Philosophy of Care

At Millbrook Manor, we believe that every person deserves to be treated with dignity, respect, kindness and compassion.

Our philosophy extends beyond clinical and personal care. We consider the whole person and seek to support physical, emotional, social and psychological well-being.

We take time to understand each resident's individual needs and preferences, supporting independence wherever possible while providing appropriate assistance and reassurance.

Person-centred care: Each resident's care and support should reflect their individual needs, wishes, abilities and personal circumstances.

Our Services

Millbrook Manor provides residential and nursing care designed to meet the needs of adults requiring ongoing support. Our services include:

Long-Term Residential Care

Comprehensive day-to-day support for residents who require ongoing assistance with care needs, physical frailty or age-related conditions.

Specialist Dementia Care

Supportive and familiar care environments designed to promote comfort, dignity, security and quality of life for people living with dementia or other cognitive impairments.

Palliative & End-of-Life Care

Compassionate and dignified care focused on comfort, symptom management and support for residents and their families.

Adult Support Services

Individualised care and support for adults aged 18 and over who require residential nursing, personal or rehabilitative assistance.

Why Choose Millbrook Manor?

24/7 Professional Nursing

Nursing and healthcare support is available around the clock to assist residents with their care needs.

A Vibrant Community

We encourage social interaction, activities, therapeutic engagement and meaningful connections within the home and wider community.

Comfortable Facilities

Our environment is designed with comfort, accessibility, safety and resident well-being in mind, including communal and quiet spaces.

Nutritional Support

Meals are planned with residents' dietary requirements, nutritional needs and individual preferences in mind.

Supporting Independence & Well-Being

We believe that receiving residential care should not mean losing independence or personal choice. Our team works with residents and, where appropriate, their families and representatives to understand individual preferences and support personal routines.

We encourage residents to participate in activities and decisions concerning their daily lives wherever they are able and willing to do so.

Through respectful communication, compassionate support and individual care planning, we strive to create an environment where residents feel valued, listened to and at home.

We Welcome You to Visit

We invite prospective residents, families and representatives to visit Millbrook Manor and learn more about our approach to residential care, our facilities and the support we provide.
