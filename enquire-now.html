Welcome to Millbrook Manor Nursing Home

Millbrook Manor Nursing Home is a private residential care facility in the peaceful surroundings of Saggart, County Dublin. We are dedicated to providing a safe, warm and welcoming environment where residents can receive compassionate, person-centred care.

Our approach is centred on dignity, respect and individual wellbeing. We take the time to understand each resident's needs, preferences and personal circumstances so that their care can be tailored appropriately.

If you are considering care for yourself or a family member, please complete the enquiry form below. A member of our team can then respond to your enquiry and discuss your requirements.
