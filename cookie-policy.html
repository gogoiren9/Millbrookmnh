Cookie Policy

Information about how cookies and similar technologies may be used when you visit the Millbrook Manor Nursing Home website.

Last Updated: 27 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Millbrook Manor Nursing Home ("Millbrook Manor", "we", "us" or "our") may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit or interact with our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be placed on your computer, tablet or mobile device when you visit a website.

They can allow a website to recognise a device, remember certain information and help provide or improve website functionality.

2. How We May Use Cookies

Depending on how the website is configured, cookies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Helping the website operate correctly.
  • Remembering certain preferences or settings.
  • Understanding how visitors use the website.
  • Improving website performance and usability.
  • Supporting security and preventing misuse.
  • Supporting embedded services or third-party website features.

3. Types of Cookies

Cookies can generally be grouped into several categories. The exact cookies used may depend on the services and features active on the website at a particular time.

Essential cookies may be necessary for certain website functions to operate correctly.

Preference cookies may remember choices or settings made by a visitor.

Analytics cookies may help us understand how visitors use the website, where such analytics services are enabled.

Third-party cookies may be placed by external services that are integrated into or displayed through the website.

4. Third-Party Services

Some website features may rely on third-party services. These services may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies.

Examples may include website analytics, embedded content, maps, forms, video services or other external functionality.

Please note: Third-party services may change their cookies, technologies or privacy practices from time to time. Their own policies should be consulted for further information.

5. Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through the browser's settings.

You can generally configure your browser to notify you when a cookie is being placed, block certain cookies or delete cookies that have already been stored.

If you disable cookies, some website features may not function as intended.

6. Browser Cookie Controls

Cookie controls are normally available within your browser's privacy, security or settings menu.

Because browser settings vary between devices and software providers, please refer to your browser's own help information for instructions on managing cookies.

7. Cookies and Personal Information

A cookie does not necessarily identify you personally. However, certain information collected through cookies or similar technologies may potentially be associated with other information.

Where personal information is collected through our website, it is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy .

8. Website Security

Cookies or similar technologies may be used by website infrastructure or security services to help protect the website against malicious activity, abuse or unauthorised access.

9. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technologies we use, applicable requirements or our practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or how cookies may be used on our website, you can contact Millbrook Manor Nursing Home.
